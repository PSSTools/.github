## What this is, and what it is not

This is the Apache Software Foundation's Individual Contributor License Agreement v2.0, with
the counterparty changed and **one section added**. It is a *license*, not an assignment —
you keep the copyright in everything you write, and you may use your own contributions for
any other purpose, including in proprietary work.

**The three differences from the stock Apache ICLA v2.0**, so a reviewer can diff quickly:

1. **The counterparty is a person, not a foundation.** "the Foundation" is replaced
   throughout by "the Maintainer", defined below as Matthew Ballance. psstools is a GitHub
   organization, which is a namespace, not a legal person, and cannot be a party to a
   contract.
2. **Section 9 is new** — stewardship, relicensing, and assignment. This is the only
   substantive addition, and the reason it exists is explained inside it.
3. Section 3's patent-termination sentence, section 6's warranty disclaimer, and sections
   1, 2, 4, 5, 7 and 8 are **unchanged** from the stock text apart from the counterparty
   substitution.

Nothing in this document is legal advice. If you are signing on behalf of anyone but
yourself, read section 4 carefully — it is the part that most often turns out not to hold.

**Why it exists.** PSS is an Accellera standard. The best plausible outcome for a tool like
`pssparser` or `pssc` is that it outlives its current maintainer under a standards body or a
foundation. Apache-2.0 plus sign-off does not make that possible without tracking down every
contributor individually, years after the fact, which in practice is how such moves die.
Paragraphs (a)–(c) keep that door open; paragraph (d) is what keeps the door from swinging
the other way.

## How to sign

Fill in the block below and **email it to `matt.ballance@gmail.com`** with the subject
`ICLA: <your name>`. Sending it from the address you commit with is itself the signature —
no scanner, no printer, no third-party service.

You may also post the completed block as a comment on the pull request, if you would rather
it be part of the public record. Either is accepted.

```

I have read the Agreement at
https://github.com/psstools/.github/blob/main/ICLA.md
and I agree to its terms.

  Full name:        
  Git email(s):     
  GitHub username:  
  Postal address:   
  Country:          
  Employer:         (or "none" / "not contributing on company time")
  Date:             
```

**Employer:** if you name one, expect to be asked about section 4 before anything merges.
That is not an obstacle course — it is much cheaper to settle before the code is written than
after.

Signed agreements are recorded in [`CONTRIBUTORS.md`](CONTRIBUTORS.md). Your email and postal
address are kept privately by the Maintainer and are not published; what appears in
`CONTRIBUTORS.md` is your name, a contact you are happy to have public, and the fact that an
agreement is on file.
